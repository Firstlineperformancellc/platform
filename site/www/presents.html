Questions

What is a Present?

A short personal video message recorded by an FLP athlete for someone specific: a birthday, a big game, a comeback, a thank-you to a coach, a team shout-out. The athlete records it on their phone and FLP delivers it as a private link.

Who can order one?

Any adult. Parents with an FLP account can order from their dashboard; everyone else orders with an email address and confirms they are 18 or over. No account needed. The request is one-way: the athlete sees the recipient's first name, the occasion and your notes, never your contact details.

Is it safe for young players?

Every video made for a recipient under 18 is checked by FLP before it is delivered. Requests are one-way, recipients are identified by first name only, and there is no messaging between the athlete and the recipient.

What if it isn't delivered?

If the athlete doesn't deliver within their stated time, you're refunded automatically. Delivered videos aren't refundable, except for a real problem reported within 7 days from your private link, which FLP reviews.

What does it cost?

Each athlete sets their own price. It's shown on their tile and again before you pay. You're charged once at checkout through Stripe; FLP never holds your card details.
