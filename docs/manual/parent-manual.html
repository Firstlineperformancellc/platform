Section 1

What FLP is and who the mentors are

First Line Performance connects youth hockey players and their families with current and former NCAA, PWHL and professional players. You pick the mentor, they watch your youth athlete's own game film, and they tell them what they see.

The two things you can order

  • A breakdown. You upload game film of your youth athlete and choose an FLP Mentor. The mentor watches the film, records a video walking through it, and fills in a Player Development Worksheet. You get the video and a branded PDF of the worksheet, usually within 72 hours of the mentor accepting the job.
  • A Film Room session. A recorded live video call, 30 or 60 minutes, where the mentor and your youth athlete go through film together. You book inside the mentor's weekly openings, and a short written recap follows within a day.

There is also First Line Presents, a personal video message from an FLP athlete for a birthday or a big game. It's separate from mentoring; Section 8 covers it.

Who the mentors are

Every FLP Mentor applied to First Line Performance, signed an agreement, and was reviewed by the FLP team before being listed. Mentors are grouped into levels by the highest level they played (for example Pro, PWHL, NCAA), and the price of a breakdown or a Film Room follows the level, so every mentor at a level costs the same. A mentor's page shows where they played, what they're best at teaching, who they want to help, their career history, and what other parents said.

Who is who

  • Parent or guardian. You. The account is yours; you order, pay, rate, and can join any session.
  • Youth athlete. Your player. A profile under your account, never a login of their own.
  • FLP Mentor. The athlete who reviews the film and runs the sessions.
  • FLP. The First Line Performance team (Alex and Bryan). They approve mentors, review audits, handle refunds, and answer support.

Where it all happens

In your browser at app.firstlineperform.com, on a laptop or a phone. Mobile apps are planned; the website works on any device today. Email is how FLP keeps you posted on orders, bookings, and deliveries, so add firstlineperform.com to your safe senders.

Section 2

Your account and your youth athlete

Parents own the account

Every family account belongs to a parent or guardian. Your youth athlete is a profile under your account: a name, age group, position, level and team. They never get a login, a password, or a way to message anyone. Everything a mentor sees or sends goes through you.

Creating your account

  1. Go to app.firstlineperform.com and choose Create an account, then I'm a parent.
  2. Enter your name, your email, and a password of at least 8 characters. Creating the account means you agree to FLP's Terms and Privacy Policy, linked on the form.
  3. FLP emails you a confirmation link. Open it within 24 hours and you're signed in. If it doesn't arrive in a couple of minutes, check spam, then press Resend the email.

Signing in

  • Sign in with your email and password.
  • Prefer not to type a password? Enter your email and press Email me a sign-in link. The link signs you in on its own; it lasts 24 hours.
  • Forgot the password? Forgot your password? emails you a link to set a new one.
  • If sign-in says your email isn't confirmed yet, press Send me a new confirmation link, open the email, and you're in.

Your dashboard

Signing in opens your dashboard. Across the top: Account, Support, and Sign out. Below your name: this guide, then three cards, Your youth athletes, Order a breakdown (with Browse FLP Mentors and Your breakdowns), and Film Room (with Book a Film Room and Your sessions). If FLP has added a short "How FLP works" video, it plays near the top.

Adding your youth athlete

Do this once, before your first order. Open Your youth athletes, then Add a youth athlete:

FieldNotes
First nameRequired. This is what the mentor sees.
Last nameShown to the mentor as an initial only.
Age groupPick one (for example 10U, 12U, 14U).
PositionForward, defense, or goalie. Mentors are listed by the positions they review, and goalies get their own focus areas.

Press Add youth athlete. You can add more than one player; each has their own orders, sessions, and Development Log. Edit on a player's card changes any of this later.

Only a first name and last initial ever appear to an FLP Mentor. Age group, position, level, and what you asked them to look at are the rest of what a mentor sees about your youth athlete.

Your contact details

Account on the dashboard holds your full name, phone, and mailing address. This is how FLP reaches you if they need to; nothing here is shown to mentors. Your sign-in email is shown there too. To change it, write to support (Section 10).

Section 3

Ordering a breakdown

Press Order a breakdown on the dashboard. The order is a four-step wizard; the film upload comes right after you pay.

Before you start: browsing mentors

Browse FLP Mentors opens the marketplace. Filter by Position and, where there is more than one, Level. Each tile shows the mentor's photo, name, level, team, whether they're Available or on Waitlist (at capacity), their average turnaround, and their rating. Open a tile to read their page: their bio, what they're best at teaching, who they want to help, an intro video if they recorded one, their career history, and what other parents said. Order a breakdown on a mentor's page starts the wizard with that mentor already chosen. A few mentors show "Pricing by arrangement"; for those, press Contact FLP for pricing and FLP sets it up with you.

Step 1: Youth athlete

Pick who the breakdown is for. If you have one player, they're selected already. Add another youth athlete is here too.

Step 2: What to look at

  • What should the mentor look at? Tick everything that applies. Skaters and goalies get different lists (for example hockey IQ, skating, shooting, positioning; or rebound control, angle play, puck tracking).
  • Notes for the mentor. What's the situation? Jersey number and color helps the mentor find your player on the ice. Mention anything you want them to watch for.
  • Already on YouTube? Paste the link here (unlisted is fine) and you can skip the file upload after paying. Leave it blank to upload the file instead.

Step 3: Choose your FLP Mentor

  1. The mentors who review your player's position are shown as tiles. Click one to make them your First choice.
  2. Then click another to make them your Second choice, in case your first can't take the job in time. The two must be different mentors. The toggle above the tiles lets you switch which one you're picking.
  3. If your first choice is at capacity, you're asked how many days to wait for them (3 is suggested) before the job goes to your second choice.

Step 4: Review and pay

The summary shows your youth athlete, what you asked to look at, your mentor (and the waitlist hold, if any), your second choice, the turnaround (72 hours from the moment the mentor accepts), and the total. Press Pay. You're taken to Stripe, the payments company FLP uses, to enter your card. FLP never sees or stores your card details, and Stripe emails you a receipt. You're charged now, even if you're joining a waitlist. If you leave Stripe without paying, nothing is charged and the order isn't placed.

What happens after you pay

You land on your order page, which says Payment received. One more step: upload the game film (Section 4). From then on, Where it stands on the order page tracks the job: Paid, Film uploaded, Mentor offered, Accepted and in progress, Breakdown delivered. Your breakdowns on the dashboard lists every order with its status.

If a mentor declines or doesn't answer within 48 hours, the job moves to your second choice automatically, with the same 48 hours. You don't need to do anything; the order page and your email keep up.
Section 4

Uploading the game film

The mentor can't start until they have film. Upload it right after paying, from the order page or from Upload film on Your breakdowns.

What works

  • Any video from a phone works.
  • MP4 or MOV is ideal. Any length.
  • A full game is usually 1 to 4 GB and takes 5 to 30 minutes on home wifi.
  • Keep this tab open until the bar reaches 100%. The upload runs in this browser tab.
  • If the connection drops, it pauses and resumes on its own.
  • Or paste a YouTube link on the order step instead.

Full games are fine; the bigger the better. The mentor would rather see a whole game than a highlight reel. If FLP has added a 30-second walkthrough video, it plays on the upload page.

Uploading

  1. On the order page press Upload film, then Choose a video file and pick the game.
  2. Watch the bar. You can Pause and Resume. If you go offline, a pill says Offline, will resume and it picks up where it left off.
  3. At 100% the video shows Processing for a few minutes, then Ready, and you can play it back. Press Back to your order.

Your mentor gets the film as soon as it has processed, but only once they've accepted the job. Until then nobody but you and FLP can open it.

If something goes wrong

What you seeWhat to do
"That's a .pdf" (or another extension) "Send the game as a video file"The file isn't a video. Export the game as MP4 or MOV and try again.
"That file is over 20 GB"Export a smaller version or split the game into two files.
"The video could not be processed. Try a different file."The file reached us but couldn't be read. Re-export it from your phone or camera app and upload again.
"Uploaded, but attaching it to the order failed"The video is safe. Write to support with your order and FLP will attach it.
"Use a browser for now"Uploading from the mobile app arrives with the native build. Open app.firstlineperform.com in a browser on the same phone or on a laptop.

YouTube instead

If the game is already on YouTube, paste the link on the "What to look at" step of the order. Unlisted videos work. The order page then shows a YouTube pill instead of an upload card, and the mentor records their breakdown from there.

Section 5

What you get back

When the mentor delivers, you're emailed Your breakdown is ready with the worksheet PDF attached, and the order page gains a Your breakdown card.

The recorded breakdown

The mentor's video plays right on the order page, as many times as you like. FLP asks every mentor to follow the same structure, about 10 to 15 minutes: a short introduction, three strengths, three areas to improve with how to fix them, hockey IQ tips from their level, a practice focus for the next month, and encouragement to close. Watch it together with your youth athlete.

The Player Development Worksheet

The written half of the breakdown, shown on the order page and available as a branded PDF with Download PDF. It has the same backbone as the video, so the family reads what they just heard:

PartWhat's in it
StrengthsWhat your player did well, in specifics.
Areas to improveThe two or three things that would change their game most.
Game situations from the filmOne block per clip: the time and period, the situation, what happened, what to improve, and a one-line key takeaway. At least one clip; three is the standard.
Recommended workouts and drillsOne block per drill: the area it works on, the drill, a description, how often, and notes.
Next stepsThe action plan for the next couple of weeks, in order.
Additional notesAnything else the mentor wants you to have.

Go through it live

For 14 days after delivery, the same card offers Book the add-on: a 30-minute Film Room with the same mentor at the add-on price. They pull up this film and walk your youth athlete through it. Section 6 covers how sessions work.

The Development Log

Every delivered breakdown and every Film Room recap is filed under your youth athlete, oldest first, so you can read the arc from the first order on. Open it from Development Log on their card under Your youth athletes. Breakdown entries show the full worksheet and your rating; session entries show the mentor's takeaways, drills, and next step. It's yours for as long as you have the account, and it's a useful thing to show the next coach.

Section 6

Film Room sessions

A Film Room is a live, recorded video call where an FLP Mentor and your youth athlete go through film together. Sessions are 30 or 60 minutes and are booked from the mentor's page.

Booking

  1. Open the mentor's page (Book a Film Room on the dashboard takes you to the marketplace) and find Film Room with ….
  2. Pick the Session: 30 or 60 minutes, with the price. If you came from a delivered breakdown, the add-on option is at the top.
  3. Pick the Youth athlete.
  4. Pick a time. The openings are the mentor's own weekly windows, shown in your time zone, at least 12 hours from now and up to 14 days out. If the mentor hasn't opened any times, check back soon or pick another mentor.
  5. Say what the session should focus on (optional), and choose Who's on the call: Just my youth athlete or I'll sit in too.
  6. Press Book and pay through Stripe. The slot is held while you pay.

Confirmation

The mentor has 24 hours to confirm. Your session shows Waiting on the mentor until they do, then Scheduled, and you're emailed Your Film Room is confirmed. If they can't make it, or don't answer in time, the booking is cancelled and you're refunded in full, automatically. Reminders come by email the day before and one hour before.

Season Arc

Below the booking form, most mentors offer a Season Arc: four 30-minute Film Rooms with the same mentor and the same youth athlete over eight weeks, bought as a pack. After you buy one, the booking form offers Pay with Season Arc credit, and Your sessions shows how many are left and the date to use them by, with Book the next one.

Joining

  • Open the session from Your sessions. Join the room appears 15 minutes before the start and opens the video room in a new tab. Your youth athlete joins from your device; there is no separate login for them.
  • You may join at any time, whether or not you said you'd sit in. That's always your choice.
  • Recording is always on. It starts when the mentor joins and nobody can turn it off; both sides are told on joining. Recordings are kept by FLP for 90 days for quality and safety review. They aren't published or sent to the mentor.
  • If you drop, Rejoin the room puts you back in.

Afterwards: the recap and your rating

Within 24 hours the mentor writes a recap: three takeaways, one or two drills, and one next step. It lands on the session page, in your youth athlete's Development Log, and in your inbox as Your Film Room recap is in. The session page then asks you to rate the session from 1 to 5 stars, with an optional testimonial (Section 7).

Section 7

Ratings and the Quality Control Audit

Rating a breakdown or a session

After a breakdown is delivered, and after a Film Room completes, the page asks Rate this breakdown (or session): pick 1 to 5 stars and, if you like, write a testimonial about what your youth athlete got out of it. Press Submit rating.

  • Ratings of 4 and 5 stars publish on their own to the mentor's page, under What parents say, along with your testimonial and your youth athlete's age group and position.
  • Ratings of 3 stars and under go to FLP first. FLP reads them and decides whether to publish; either way they count on the mentor's internal record.
  • You always see your own rating on your order or session page, whatever its status.

Mentors' tiles show their average rating and how many ratings it's built on, so honest ratings help the next family choose.

The Quality Control Audit

If a breakdown fell short, you can file a Quality Control Audit within 7 days of delivery. On the order page, under your rating, press Not what you expected?, tell FLP what was wrong, and press File the audit.

  • Alex or Bryan at FLP watch the breakdown, read the worksheet, and read your reason.
  • While the audit is open, the mentor's payment for that job is on hold.
  • The outcome is one of: a full refund, a partial refund, a re-review by another mentor (the job is offered to a different FLP Mentor at no extra cost), or the audit is closed with notes when the work meets FLP's standard. You're emailed Your Quality Control Audit was resolved, and the order page shows the result.

Only one audit can be open on a breakdown at a time. For a concern about a Film Room session, write to support (Section 10); FLP can review the recording and refund a session the same way.

Section 8

First Line Presents

A personal video message from an FLP athlete, recorded on their phone, for a birthday, a big game, a comeback, or a thank-you to a coach. It's separate from mentoring and sits at the bottom of your dashboard.

Ordering one

  1. Press Browse athletes on the dashboard's First Line Presents card (or Request a video on a mentor's page). Each athlete sets their own price and delivery time, 3, 5 or 7 days.
  2. Fill in the request: who it's for (first name only), whether they are under 18, the occasion, who it's from, what the athlete should say (up to 500 characters), and the team if it matters. Leave out phone numbers, emails, links and handles; the form refuses them, because the athlete only records a video.
  3. Pay through Stripe. The confirmation shows the date the athlete has to deliver by.

Anyone 18 or over can order one, with or without an FLP account. Yours are listed under My videos.

Under-18 review

Every video made for someone under 18 is checked by FLP before it's delivered, usually within a day. The request page says Being checked by FLP while that happens. FLP's review time never counts against the athlete's deadline.

Delivery and the private link

When the video is released you're emailed a private link. The page plays the video, has Download for the MP4, and Copy private link so you can send it to the recipient's family. The link is unguessable and never shows the athlete's contact details. You can rate the video from 1 to 5 stars and change the rating any time.

Refunds and problems

  • If the athlete declines, or doesn't deliver by the due date, you're refunded in full automatically.
  • After delivery, Report a problem on the video page within 7 days: wrong name, wrong occasion, won't play. A person at FLP looks at it and can refund in part or in full. A video that simply wasn't to taste isn't refundable.
  • If you leave Stripe without paying, nothing is charged.
Section 9

Safety and privacy

Every youth athlete on FLP is a minor. These rules are built into the platform, not left to policy.

  • Parents own the account. Your youth athlete never has a login. Every order, booking, payment, and rating comes from you.
  • No direct messaging. There is no chat, inbox, or contact exchange between mentors and families. A mentor hears from you only through the notes on an order or a booking, and you hear from them through the breakdown, the worksheet, the session, and the recap. Mentors are contractually barred from contacting a family outside FLP; if one does, tell support.
  • What a mentor sees about your player. First name and last initial, age group, position, level, and what you asked them to look at. Never your name, email, phone, or address.
  • Who can see the film. Game film and breakdown videos are private, signed video. Only you, the mentor on the job once they've accepted it, and FLP can open them; a copied link doesn't work for anyone else. Mentors may use the film only to do the job.
  • Every session is recorded. Recording starts when the mentor joins and can't be stopped. FLP keeps recordings for 90 days for quality and safety review. You may join any session at any time.
  • Mentors are reviewed. Every FLP Mentor applied, signed an agreement covering conduct and safety, and was approved by FLP before being listed. FLP can suspend or remove a mentor.
  • First Line Presents. Requests are first-name only, contact details are refused, and every video for an under-18 recipient is checked by FLP before delivery.
If anything worries you about a mentor's conduct or your youth athlete's safety, tell FLP straight away: Support in the app or support@firstlineperform.com. FLP reads every message, can review the recording, and acts on it.

FLP's Terms and Privacy Policy are at firstlineperform.com.

Section 12

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to be on the Film Room call?
No, that's your choice. You say at booking whether you'll sit in, and you can join any session at any time regardless. Every session is recorded either way.
What does the mentor see about my youth athlete?
First name and last initial, age group, position, level, and what you asked them to look at. The film opens for them only after they accept the job. They never see your name or contact details.
Can I order for more than one child?
Yes. Add each youth athlete under Your youth athletes. Every order, session, and Development Log is filed under the right player.
I paid but haven't uploaded the film yet. Is that a problem?
Only that the mentor can't start. Upload it as soon as you can from the order page or from Upload film on Your breakdowns; the mentor gets it the moment it has processed.
How do I cancel a Film Room, and do I get my money back?
Open the session under Your sessions and press Cancel. More than 24 hours before, it's a full refund. Inside 24 hours of a confirmed session there's no refund, because the mentor kept the time for you. The button tells you which before you press it.
What if the mentor doesn't show up?
Ten minutes after the start, press "didn't show" on the session page. You're refunded in full and FLP follows up with the mentor. A session the mentor never joined is refunded automatically a day after its end even if you don't press it.
Can I watch the recording of a Film Room afterwards?
No. Recordings are kept by FLP for 90 days for quality and safety review and aren't published. What you keep is the mentor's recap, on the session page and in the Development Log.
Why isn't my review on the mentor's page?
Ratings of 4 and 5 stars publish on their own. Ratings of 3 and under go to FLP first, who decide whether to publish. You always see your own rating on your order or session page.
How do refunds come back?
To the card you paid with, through Stripe, and you're emailed when one is issued. It can take a few business days to show on your statement.
Where do I find this guide again?
On your dashboard under Parent Guide, from "Read the Parent Guide" in the order wizard, and from the Support page.
